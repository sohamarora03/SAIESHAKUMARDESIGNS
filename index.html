Hi, I'm Saiesha

Two pixels to the left, until it feels right.

I care about colour, layout, type and alignment more than is probably reasonable. Every tiny detail matters to me, and I will happily spend far too long nudging something over because it just does not sit right yet. I enjoy every minute of it.

What pulled me toward design in the first place is how calm it makes a chaotic day feel. Starting with a blank canvas and slowly watching everything click into place is one of my favourite feelings, every time.

Lately I am most drawn to work that holds a memory: an invite, a menu, a keepsake someone still has years later. I like knowing that something I designed could become part of how someone remembers a day that mattered to them.

I am still early in my design journey, but I care deeply about the work I make, and I am always looking to learn from a team that will push me further.
